# Learning goals

## Long-term outcome

Communicate fluently and naturally in common daily-life, workplace, and travel situations, with enough reading and writing ability to handle practical information and professional communication confidently.

## Target capabilities

### Daily life

- Start, maintain, and close casual conversations naturally.
- Express opinions, experiences, preferences, and follow-up questions without relying on direct Chinese translation.
- Use common collocations and socially appropriate phrasing.

### Workplace

- Participate in routine discussions, clarification, status updates, problem-solving, and written chat.
- Write clear messages, emails, summaries, and feedback with an appropriate register.
- Explain technical or complex ideas in organized, understandable English.

### Travel

- Handle transportation, accommodation, dining, shopping, directions, and unexpected problems.
- Read practical travel information and ask effective follow-up questions.

## Initial practice balance

- Daily life: 40%
- Workplace: 40%
- Travel: 20%

Treat these as starting weights, not quotas. Adapt them when session history or the learner's request shows a different need.

## Checkpoints

1. Complete a baseline covering conversation, reading, and writing.
2. Review recurring patterns after the first 10 completed sessions.
3. Produce a broader progress analysis monthly or when the learner requests it.
