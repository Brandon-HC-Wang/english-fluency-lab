# Learning goals

## Long-term outcome

Communicate fluently and naturally in common daily-life, workplace, and travel situations, with enough reading and writing ability to handle practical information and professional communication confidently.

## Target capabilities

### Daily life

- Start, maintain, and close casual conversations naturally.
- Express opinions, experiences, preferences, and follow-up questions without relying on direct Chinese translation.
- Use common collocations and socially appropriate phrasing.

### Workplace

- Participate in routine discussions, clarification, status updates, problem-solving, and written chat.
- Write clear messages, emails, summaries, and feedback with an appropriate register.
- Explain technical or complex ideas in organized, understandable English.

### Travel

- Handle transportation, accommodation, dining, shopping, directions, and unexpected problems.
- Read practical travel information and ask effective follow-up questions.

## Initial practice balance

- Daily life: 40%
- Workplace: 40%
- Travel: 20%

Treat these as starting weights, not quotas. Adapt them when session history or the learner's request shows a different need.

## Practice frequency and length

- Practice on demand whenever the learner wants to; no fixed weekly frequency or quota.
- General conversation has no default time limit. Continue until the learner says `結束這次的聊天` or an unambiguous equivalent, then summarize and save the record.
- Quick mode ends automatically after two or three learner replies, then summarizes and saves the record.
- Honor an explicit length requested for the current activity, such as five questions. A stated time budget helps keep the activity compact; it does not require abruptly cutting off an unfinished learner response.

## Review integration

- Keep due review items in the queue until the learner returns; a due date does not require starting a practice session or catching up on every overdue item.
- At the next practice, prioritize due items in review or quiz mode. In general conversation, bring in a small number only when they fit the requested scenario naturally.
- Preserve the learner's chosen activity and length rather than turning every practice into a review session.
- Test retrieval in fresh contexts without showing the target first. Count only independent correct production, and require evidence across separate sessions before treating an item as mastered.
- Use the defaults in [review-policy.md](../.agents/skills/english-coach/references/review-policy.md) for intervals and mastery; adjust them if the learner requests a different approach.

## Checkpoints

1. Complete a baseline covering conversation, reading, and writing.
2. Review recurring patterns after the first 10 completed sessions.
3. Produce a broader progress analysis monthly or when the learner requests it.
