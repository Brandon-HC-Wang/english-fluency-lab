# Quick practice: weekend plans

## Context

- Scenario: 週五下午，與朋友聊週末計畫。週五是角色情境，不是實際練習日期。
- Learner role: 分享週末計畫的朋友
- Coach role: 關心近況的朋友
- Practice focus: 自然描述休息、下班狀態與休閒活動
- Completion: 三次英文回覆後自動結束；沒有既有複習項目。

## Dialogue

### Coach opening

Hey! Got any plans for the weekend?

### Turn 1

#### Learner — original

Actually not yet, I have no idea what I want to do in the weekend, maybe just veg out on the bed.

#### Coaching

- Classification: 錯誤（週末時間表達）；不自然（休息地點表達）
- Natural revision: Actually, not yet. I have no idea what I want to do this weekend. Maybe I’ll just veg out in bed.
- 中文說明: `this weekend` 表達「這個週末」；`in bed` 表達「躺在床上休息」。`veg out` 在這個情境用得自然，有「放空、耍廢」的意思。
- Evidence: `veg out` 在任何教練示範之前由學習者自行使用。

#### Coach — English reply

That sounds pretty relaxing. Has it been a busy week, or are you just in the mood for a quiet weekend?

### Turn 2

#### Learner — original

it's really busy week literally, I felt burn out every time I left from office. that's why I just want to veg out this weekend.

#### Coaching

- Classification: 錯誤
- Natural revision: It’s been a really busy week, literally. I felt burned out every time I left the office. That’s why I just want to veg out this weekend.
- 中文說明: `burned out` 表達「身心疲憊、耗盡精力」；`left the office` 表達「離開辦公室」。對話中未展開文法規則說明。
- Native alternative for the second sentence: I’ve been feeling burned out after work every day.
- Evidence: 使用 `that's why` 連結工作疲憊與想休息的原因；這次使用 `this weekend` 在前一回合示範之後，不視為獨立複習成功或熟練證據。

#### Coach — English reply

I get that. A low-key weekend sounds like just what you need. What’s your favorite way to unwind at home?

### Turn 3

#### Learner — original

I like to read the Japanese light novels, or accompany with my wife and daughter.

#### Coaching

- Classification: 錯誤（accompany with）；不自然（此處泛指小說的表達）
- Natural revision: I like to read Japanese light novels or spend time with my wife and daughter.
- 中文說明: `spend time with my wife and daughter` 自然表達「陪太太和女兒」。保留原本正確的 `I like to read`，不為改寫而改寫。

#### Coach — English reply

Not applicable: quick mode ended automatically after the third learner reply. No further in-character reply was given.

## Session summary

### Strengths

- 能直接回應週末計畫、工作近況與休閒偏好，三次回覆都能傳達主要意思。
- 自行使用 `veg out` 表達放空休息。
- 使用 `that's why` 說明想休息的原因。

### High-value corrections

- `in the weekend` → `this weekend`: 表達「這個週末」。
- `veg out on the bed` → `veg out in bed`: 自然表達躺在床上休息。
- `felt burn out` → `felt burned out`: 表達感到身心疲憊。
- `left from office` → `left the office`: 表達離開辦公室。
- `accompany with my wife and daughter` → `spend time with my wife and daughter`: 在休閒情境表達陪家人。

### Useful expressions

- `veg out`: 放空、耍廢；學習者自行使用。
- `feel burned out`: 感到身心疲憊、耗盡精力；本次教練修正。
- `spend time with someone`: 陪某人、與某人相處；本次教練修正。
- `a low-key weekend`: 輕鬆、沒有太多安排的週末；由教練提供，尚未觀察學習者自行使用。
- `unwind`: 放鬆、紓解壓力；由教練提供，尚未觀察學習者自行使用。
